iPhone Camera Setup

Follow these steps to use your iPhone as a SecCam camera.

1

Download the CA Certificate

Open this link in Safari on your iPhone:
Download Certificate Open /ca.crt

Tap Allow when prompted to download the profile.

2

Install the Profile

Go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management (or Profiles on older iOS). Tap the downloaded SecCam Local CA profile and install it.
3

Enable Full Trust

Go to Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings. Under ENABLE FULL TRUST FOR ROOT CERTIFICATES, turn on SecCam Local CA.
4

Open the Camera Page

In Safari, open:
Open Camera Page

The URL is — it uses your current hostname.

Desktop viewers: If you open the camera or dashboard in a desktop browser, you'll see a certificate warning. Either install and trust the SecCam Local CA certificate in your OS/browser, or accept the warning to proceed.
